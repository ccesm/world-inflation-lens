**Research → AI Infrastructure & Financing** (`#/research/ai-infrastructure`)
presents fixed-vintage project evidence qualified as of **2026-10-07**. Observation,
source publication and source-vintage availability dates remain separate. The
exact four sections are Project landscape, How the buildout is financed, Power &
grid, and What this data can and cannot show.

- Microsoft: Fairwater 1 and Quincy.
- Alphabet: zero qualified cards, with an explicit evidence-boundary explanation.
- Amazon: Warren County.
- Meta: Hyperion and El Paso.
- Oracle: Jupiter.
- Polaris Forge 1: independent comparative example outside the five companies.

All seven financing structures remain **UNKNOWN / not yet publicly qualified**.
Unknown does not mean no financing, self-funding, no debt or no leverage. No
project-specific financing amounts, ownership percentages or excluded legal
claims are published.

Capacity retains native unit, definition, scope, qualifier and dates. Planned is
not operational; contracted is not installed. Polaris' 400 MW contracted and
175 MW reported live capacity remain separate. Utility generation/grid plans are
separate from campus capacity, consumption and investment. Fairwater's dated
construction disclosure retains its completion-timeline caveat.

The prominent **DO NOT ADD** disclosure explains that project cost, corporate
CapEx, project debt, leases, guarantees and maximum exposure may describe layers
of the same infrastructure; addition requires proven non-overlapping scopes and
asset identities. Guarantees and maximum exposure are not debt, expected loss or
expected payment. No aggregate dollars, ranking, AI ROI, financing/hidden-leverage
score or systemic-risk score is created.

AI CapEx & Monetization remains company-level accounting/monetization research;
this page is project-level physical status, native capacity and power evidence.
Contextual links do not imply a reconciliation to corporate CapEx. AI Labor
Observation Mode and Signal Engine methodology/conclusions remain unchanged.
No new workflow, live refresh, runtime economic API or email is introduced.
