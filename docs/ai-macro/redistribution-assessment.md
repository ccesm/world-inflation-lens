# D1 numeric data redistribution assessment

Reviewed 2026-10-10. Classification is limited to a noncommercial educational/research preview, properly attributed, with World Inflation Lens formatting/translation and no implication of endorsement. Authentication and redistribution rights are separate checks. No raw document archive is republished.

| Dataset bundle | Classification | Basis and limits |
|---|---|---|
| EIA_STATE; EIA_GAS | ALLOWED | EIA-generated numeric observations; [EIA reuse policy](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) permits distribution with dated acknowledgment. Protected contributed material is excluded; Chinese explanations belong to WIL and retain official links. |
| CENSUS_DC | ALLOWED | Published Census aggregate construction statistics. [Public-use/citation policy](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html) supports reuse with attribution; [DS027](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf) describes government-work and third-party exceptions. No confidential microdata, third-party material or API permission is substituted for workbook rights. |
| PNFIC1; NCBDBIQ027S; DFII10; CPIAUCSL; PCEPILFE; OPHNFB; ULCNFB | ALLOWED | Each exact FRED series page has the source-specific Public Domain: Citation Requested tag. Producer and FRED attribution retained, with [FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/). This does not authorize copying FRED charts/logos or other protected series. The generic graph copyright banner is distinct from the numeric series tag. |
| SPF10 | ALLOWED, scoped numeric-facts assessment | [Philadelphia Fed online terms](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/about-us/privacy-notice) permit informational/educational/research use and warn about protected material/noncommercial limits. Selected aggregate numerical observations from the [official inflation forecast dataset](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/real-time-data-research/inflation-forecasts) are reformatted in WIL's own schema; no anonymous respondent microdata, narrative, original workbook layout, charts or logo are reproduced. [Copyright Office guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html) distinguishes facts from protected expression. This is a reasoned assessment for this limited display, not an explicit unrestricted workbook redistribution license or a legal opinion. Commercial use/different material requires renewed rights review. |
