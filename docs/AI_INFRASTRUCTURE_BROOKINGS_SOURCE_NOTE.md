Reviewed 2026-10-07. Source class: **SECONDARY_RESEARCH**.

- [Brookings article](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/financing-the-ai-buildout/), September 23, 2026; Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh, summary by David Skidmore.
- [Underlying BPEA conference paper](https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/4c_Van-Nieuwerburgh.pdf), internal draft September 4, 2026; cover conference September 24–25, 2026. Reviewed abstract, Sections II–IV, Figure 2 and Appendix B. This is a conference draft, not a company filing.

## Framework used

The article connects physical construction, external ownership, leases, project finance and contingent obligations. It motivates our separate asset, accounting, financing and contractual layers. Its historical comparisons provide context, not harmonized accounting series or a systemic-risk verdict.

## Estimates retained as estimates

The paper's approximately $10.3 trillion 2025–2032 investment scenario uses a $41 million/MW 2025 benchmark, 4% cost/GDP growth and construction spending weights 10/25/40/25% across four years. Completion probabilities and dates are assumed; announced capacity can fail to materialize. Its 183GW completion scenario is not an observation. Hyperion's roughly $30bn asset value, 2GW scope, financing spread and leverage interpretations require independent transaction checks. Figure 2's named subsidiary chain remains secondary-reported here. A later issuer-announced expansion is not automatically the paper's financed asset.

## Primary verification and limits

Our primary transaction and accounting checks are documented in the [Hyperion case](AI_INFRASTRUCTURE_HYPERION_CASE_STUDY.md); source assertions are in `research/ai-infrastructure-financing/data/claims.json`. No Brookings industry estimate becomes company CapEx, lease liability, project cost or recognized revenue. No paper-implied leverage ratio, additional interest cost or modeled power requirement is calculated in this repository. The article's framework is useful; legal terms, title chains and accounting recognition must be established by primary documents. Raw-source immutable archival remains pending and is explicitly identified rather than simulated.
