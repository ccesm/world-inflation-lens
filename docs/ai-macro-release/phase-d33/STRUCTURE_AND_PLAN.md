# D3.3 research publication structure and minimal plan

Owner instruction: interpret evidence, do not redistribute historical datasets. The authorized design preserves the existing hash route and four macro channels. The implementation is a new narrative publication, not a byte-identical revision of the D3.2 numerical projection. No economic figures were selected for this edition; dates, dataset identifiers, evidence labels and hashes are provenance rather than numerical findings.

## Page structure

1. **Research position:** the retained evidence shows visible infrastructure/investment activity but does not identify an AI contribution to aggregate inflation. Distinguish publication date (2026-10-17), underlying research review (2026-10-09), and actual observation/comparison dates.
2. **Four linked research sections:** Energy & Infrastructure; Investment & Financial Conditions; Productivity & Economic Growth; Inflation & Long-Term Expectations. Each contains a question, evidence-supported finding, verified observations, counterevidence, alternative explanations, unresolved question and dated method.
3. **Original-source disclosures inside each section:** original provider, official dataset name/identifier, definition/coverage, a provider landing-page link and instructions for independent retrieval. FRED IDs are explicitly distributor catalog references, not native original-provider identifiers. No automatic request, proxy, direct workbook link or source-data download. Provider links use the current browsing context.
4. **Methodology and identity:** E1 definitions/observations and E2 within-series comparisons remain distinct from unverified AI mechanisms. No cross-variable coefficient, causal estimate, forecast or aggregate score. Missingness and original research identities are preserved internally. New public content and both language copies are independently hash-bound.
