# Phase 3B case screening and selection

Reviewed 2026-10-07 before final case records. This is a bounded source-readiness screen, not an assertion that rejected projects lack documents. No financial attractiveness, ROI, credit or bubble rating is used. The reproducible scorecard is `data/phase3b/selection.json`.

Eight dimensions use 0 (not identified here), 1 (partial/context), 2 (direct primary evidence): primary source availability, entity visibility, asset specificity, financing visibility, lease visibility, power visibility, accounting visibility, scope clarity. No total or rank is calculated. Selection requires specific primary asset/developer evidence plus a defensible funding/contract archetype; unresolved site title, accounting allocation or power detail stays visible.

Hyperion is the inherited JV/project-finance anchor; it is not scored against the expansion cases. The two selected cases are deliberately different in structure, not “better” investments. Microsoft’s corporate-developed case may contain undisclosed leases or other capital sources. Applied’s developer case contains preferred equity and project securities. Archetypes are primary descriptions with secondary features, not mutually exclusive categories.

Fairwater ownership is `PRIMARY_PARTIAL`: official records identify Microsoft as the land purchaser/developer, but precise registered asset title and all corporate financing instruments have not been independently verified. It is not represented as proven 100% ownership of every asset at the site. The matrix refuses to allocate Microsoft’s company-wide CapEx to this campus.

PF1 was chosen because issuer financial statements provide a much stronger entity/lease/notes trail than an official technology collaboration alone. Its $5bn preferred facility is platform-scoped; the original up-to-$900m campus arrangement is dated historical terms. Neither is a funded-cash campus investment fact. Newer corporate disclosures and original facility terms are not concatenated into a capital-stack total.

Raw archival success was separately checked. A fetched index or press page is not treated as a deed, indenture or complete regulated service contract. Sources used only for screening remain URLs in the scorecard and are not represented as archived project evidence.
