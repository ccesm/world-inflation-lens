# World Inflation Lens v1.1.0

Release candidate prepared after the independent Public Signal Engine release review passed. This preparation does not merge or deploy the release.

## What's new

- **Public Signal Engine:** a current macro interpretation at the top of Home and the detailed **Research → Signal Engine** page (`#/research/signal-engine`).
- **Seven independent factor assessments:** four for Domestic Purchasing Power and three for International Dollar Role, each with its observation period, native confirmation window and primary evidence link.
- **Deterministic English/Chinese conclusions:** reproducible descriptions of the available evidence, with limitations visible on the research page.
- **Evidence Quality and Threshold Sensitivity:** source-quality qualifications and sensitivity notices accompany current interpretations.
- **Validated public summaries:** stale or invalid interpretation artifacts display UNAVAILABLE while economic research pages remain accessible.

The factors are organized separately, with no aggregate score or investment signal. Provisional research rules describe evidence; they do not forecast outcomes. Monthly and quarterly factors retain their own observation dates, and source data may revise.

## 更新内容

首页顶部新增当前宏观解读，并提供 **研究 → Signal Engine** 完整页面。七个独立因子分别归入国内购买力与国际美元地位，展示观测时期、确认窗口、证据质量、阈值敏感性及主要证据链接。

中英文解读采用确定性规则生成。各因子不合成为总分，也不是投资信号。解读不可用时，页面明确提示，经济数据研究仍可访问。
