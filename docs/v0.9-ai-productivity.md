## 5–6. Coverage and latest observations

See the exact table above. Quarterly dates use the first month of the quarter internally and display as `YYYY Qn`; 2026-04 means **2026 Q2**, not an April monthly observation. Commercial electricity sales currently end in May 2026 and carry a stale-observation hint on Data Health despite a more recent source update. Retrieval does not imply a new observation.

The additional **REAL_GDP_WORKER** ratio covers 1948 Q1–2026 Q2. Formula: GDPC1 / complete three-month CE16OV average × 1,000,000, yielding annualized chained-2017 USD per employed person. Both input source versions are included in CSV metadata. It is a cross-survey ratio, not BLS nonfarm output/hour. Population-control breaks, employment-composition effects and historical revisions remain visible limitations. No incomplete employment quarter is estimated.

## 8. Validation and transparent calculations

- Exact series ID/order, original agency, units, frequency, seasonal adjustment, source URLs, coverage start, minimum latest coverage and SHA metadata. Release/retrieval dates cannot be future or regress; source release cannot exceed retrieval.
- Strictly increasing periods, no duplicates or unexplained calendar gaps; quarterly dates must be January/April/July/October. Null remains null; no interpolation. Implausible negative index/level values and truncated coverage reject the candidate.
- YoY growth uses the exact 12-month lag. Quarterly annualized growth is `(level / prior-quarter level)^4 − 1`. Five-year moving average requires **all 20 consecutive quarterly YoY readings**. Long-run mean is the arithmetic mean of all available YoY readings, independent of the chart window.
- Labor charts use original BLS compensation/productivity/ULC independently. Growth subtraction is an approximation, not a replacement for official ULC; gross growth uses division. Different source update vintages and rounding can produce small discrepancies. Small multiples share dates and YoY units but have separate vertical scales.
- Two transparent interpretation checks, no score: labor easing requires positive productivity YoY and lower ULC YoY than the preceding quarter. Concurrent demand expansion requires positive YoY in real business investment, quarterly-average data-center construction and quarterly-average consumer electricity CPI. Otherwise mixed. Each group uses its latest common quarter and requires finite prior-quarter growth for every input. A quarter-start older than eight months suppresses interpretation. Monthly-to-quarterly aggregation requires all three months. Values, dates and previous rates are visible.
- Source changes are compared period-by-period. Excess withdrawals, disappeared periods, invalid metadata, download/parse failure, build or verification failure preserve/restore the complete prior data bundle and revision ledger.
