Owner-approved release preparation for PR #13. This preparation does not merge,

## What's new

**Research → AI CapEx & Monetization** (`#/research/ai-capex`) presents fixed-vintage
company evidence for **2019Q1–2026Q2**, with qualified disclosure basis
**2026-07-30**. Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta remain in neutral fixed order.

Five evidence layers remain independent:

- Investment intensity.
- Monetization evidence.
- Operating economics.
- Depreciation / accounting pressure.
- Cash conversion.

Quarterly charts, accessible source-linked tables, sparse AI run-rate/paid-seat
milestones, native cloud economics, depreciation, backlog and separate guidance
retain visible company accounting limitations. Missing/noncomparable observations
remain gaps. Disclosure dates are distinct from economic quarters; the history is
current-vintage research, not a real-time backtest.

**AI ROI remains NOT IDENTIFIED. Recognized quarterly AI-only revenue remains
UNAVAILABLE.** Run-rates are not converted to quarterly recognized revenue;
backlog is not revenue; guidance is not a realized observation. Cash-flow
compression does not establish poor AI investment returns.

AI investment, AI monetization, observed productivity and labor effects are related
but empirically distinct research questions. Their conclusions are not combined.
No ranking, composite score, scheduled refresh or runtime economic API is added.
