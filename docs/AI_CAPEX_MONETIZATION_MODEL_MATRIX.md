# AI CapEx → Monetization: Phase 1 model matrix

Research review date: 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles). Base: `e790bd03fe8549fc5008ae2fa43237daf826438c`. Research only; application version 1.1.0 and all production systems remain unchanged.

## Decision and question

**MORE ACCOUNTING / SOURCE QUALIFICATION REQUIRED.** The accounting foundation and sparse official anchors are usable for descriptive research. A continuous, automatically reproducible quarterly pipeline has not been qualified. Exact AI investment returns are not identified for any of the four companies.

The question is whether infrastructure investment eventually generates matched incremental revenue, gross profit and cash returns. Observe five distinct stages: **capital input → operational capacity → monetization → operating economics → attributable return**. Construction spending is not operational capacity; capacity is not revenue; revenue is not cash return. No stage is collapsed into a score or investment ranking.

Initial universe: Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta. Oracle is a possible future accounting benchmark. NVIDIA is an infrastructure supplier, outside this investor comparison.

### Microsoft: known, estimable, unidentified

Known: current cash investment, consolidated and cloud/segment economics, approximate asset mix, paid AI seats, AI run-rate from a different quarter, RPO and management-described capacity constraints. The July call says roughly two-thirds of CapEx was short-lived CPU/GPU assets supporting both AI and non-AI infrastructure; remainder long-lived. This is an approximate native composition disclosure, not an exact cash split or AI allocation.

Direct AI monetization anchor: **annual revenue run-rate above $37bn**, March 2026 (FY2026 Q3), plus growth 123% YoY. This is a management-defined annualized rate, not $37bn of recognized annual revenue, not a June update, and not quarterly revenue obtained by dividing by four. [Q3 release, 2026-04-29](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q3/press-release-webcast).

Product linkage: over **30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats** at the Q4 call. Do not multiply seats by a public list price: mix, discounts, bundles and realized revenue are unknown. Broader Microsoft Cloud gross margin **65%** is not an Azure-only or AI-only gross margin. Commercial RPO about **$678bn** includes OpenAI; duration and recognition guidance are demand context, not current revenue. [Q4 call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4).

Estimable: company cash investment intensity and cash FCF, segment operating margin, same-scope historical changes after source/vintage qualification. Unidentified: AI-specific cash investment, cost of serving AI revenue, incremental no-AI profit, cohort payback. Exclude Anthropic/OpenAI investment valuation results from operating AI monetization.

### Alphabet: known, estimable, unidentified

Known: cash PP&E, pure depreciation, Google Cloud revenue and operating income. Cloud's scope includes infrastructure/platform consumption, Workspace subscriptions and TPU systems; it is not AI-only revenue. Alphabet-level shared activities contain AI R&D, so Cloud operating margin does not capture all corporate AI cost. [Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000066/googexhibit991q22026.htm).

Cloud RPO **$513.9bn**, total RPO **$519.5bn**, are June 30 balances. Starting Q1 2026 the backlog includes short-term contracts formerly excluded. Preserve a definition-change flag; growth across the break is not mechanically comparable. Not revenue, receivables, guaranteed cash or an AI-only backlog. [10-Q Note 2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000071/goog-20260630.htm).

Estimable: company cash FCF, investment intensity, consolidated gross and Cloud operating margins. Unidentified: exact recognized AI revenue, AI CapEx fraction, Cloud AI gross margin and matched return. Large investment gains in other income cannot substitute for operating monetization. A numeric 2026 CapEx guidance band from an analyst summary is deliberately not accepted without an official source passage.

### Amazon: known, estimable, unidentified

Known: gross/net cash PP&E, separately disclosed finance-lease principal, CFO, AWS revenue/operating income. AWS AI annual revenue run-rate **exceeded $25bn**; its chips business separately exceeded $25bn. Keep these as separate management-defined categories; do not add them to each other or AWS revenue. Their overlap/perimeters are not reconciled. AWS reported growth **36.7%** is segment growth, not the AI growth contribution. [Q2 release](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx).

Multi-year, multi-gigawatt customer commitments and infrastructure growth explanations are capacity/demand context. Do not monetize gigawatts or treat commitments as recognized revenue. Gross PP&E additions of **63,891** in the segment note differ from cash purchases **54,208**; recognition includes lease additions and timing. A total-company quarter cannot identify AWS AI capital. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000026/amzn-20260630.htm).

Estimable: company net-cash FCF, AWS operating margin and investment changes with native definitions. Unidentified: direct AI gross profit, matched cash returns, AWS gross margin and attributable payback. Investment valuation/equity-method results remain outside operating AI returns.

### Meta: known, estimable, unidentified

Known: cash PP&E, lease-principal-inclusive CapEx/FCF, pure PP&E and server/network depreciation; advertising volume/price indicators. Ad impressions **+14% YoY** and average price **+12%** describe overall ad delivery/pricing, not measured AI contribution. Management's AI benefit attribution remains separate from those observations. Do not add their percentages or convert them into incremental AI revenue. [Q2 release](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/).

Estimable: company cash intensity/FCF and margins, advertising context, subsequent depreciation changes at comparable scope. Unidentified: distinct AI revenue/profit, fraction of ad uplift caused by AI, counterfactual advertising without AI. Core-business benefits are Level 4/5 evidence, not evidence absence: the missing element is attribution. 2026 guidance **$130–145bn** includes finance-lease principal and is a future annual range, not an observation.

## Independent analytical layers

| Layer | Safe descriptive measurements | Qualification / missing-data rule |
|---|---|---|
| Investment intensity | Native CapEx, cash PP&E/revenue, cash PP&E/CFO, exact-period changes | Preserve gross/net, lease boundary and Q/YTD/TTM scope; no summed investor league table |
| Monetization | Direct disclosed run-rate, product usage, segment revenue/growth, RPO | Management definition and evidence level retained; no lower-level conversion to revenue |
| Margin effect | Consolidated gross/operating, selected segment operating, disclosed cloud gross | Not AI-specific; costs may be allocated outside segment |
| Accounting lag | Pure depreciation, assets in service/CIP, useful-life policy events | Mixed D&A cannot enter a pure-depreciation ratio; land not depreciated |
| Cash conversion | CFO, company-defined FCF and explicitly named research cash bridge | Lease principal deducted once; working capital/taxes/SBC limit return interpretation |

Classification: **OBSERVED** for official numerical financial/product disclosures; **MANAGEMENT_ATTRIBUTION** for AI attribution, run-rate/composition/qualitative claims and guidance; **CALCULATED_FROM_DISCLOSED_VALUES** for transparent arithmetic; **MODEL_DERIVED** reserved for future scenarios; **UNAVAILABLE** is null plus reason. A run-rate can be directly disclosed while still management-defined and not GAAP revenue. Source classification is independent of disclosure usefulness.

Monetization channels remain separate: infrastructure, APIs/model consumption, subscriptions/Copilot, AI-enhanced cloud, advertising, recommendations, internal cost savings and new products. Savings are not revenue. Broad cloud and AI categories cannot become independent additive amounts without an official disjoint decomposition.
