## Fairwater 1

[Microsoft’s engineering description](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/09/18/inside-the-worlds-most-powerful-ai-datacenter/) identifies the site and buildings. [Its investment/power statement](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/09/18/made-in-wisconsin-the-worlds-most-powerful-ai-datacenter/) ties the first facility to an initial $3.3bn pledge and a separate second facility to an additional $4bn commitment; these are not realized quarterly cash PP&E. [Municipal records](https://www.mtpleasantwi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4878) provide the land-development trail. Company equipment-online evidence is separately dated.

Physical ownership/development and corporate funding are the primary archetype. Equity/debt mix, exact site capitalized balance, lease exposure, attributable revenue/utilization/cash flow and site MW remain unavailable. The company’s solar matching plan is not a statement of this site’s electrical load. A public tax-increment district’s bonds/infrastructure costs are not automatically Microsoft project borrowing.

**DO NOT ADD:** initial pledge + second-facility pledge as Fairwater1 cost; land transaction + campus pledge as two separate infrastructure investments; utility/renewable spending + Microsoft PP&E; corporate debt + project estimate. No site cost, project ROI or absence-of-leverage conclusion is inferred.

## Polaris Forge 1

[Applied’s original CoreWeave disclosure](https://ir.applieddigital.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001641172-25-013199/ex99-2.htm) and [August2025 quarterly filing](https://ir.applieddigital.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001144879-25-000069/apld-20250831.htm) identify subsidiary lessors and the separate lease tranches. [Its November2025 filing](https://ir.applieddigital.com/sec-filings/quarterly-reports/content/0001144879-26-000006/0001144879-26-000006.pdf), printed pp19–20, identifies $2.35bn 9.25% senior secured notes, issuer APLD ComputeCo LLC, maturity December15,2030, and construction/refinancing purposes. Parent completion support is not automatically a blanket parent debt guarantee. [July2026 release](https://ir.applieddigital.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001144879-26-000044/apldq426earningsreleaseasf.htm) adds the separate Building4 financing and June30 live-capacity milestone.

Developer capital funds physical infrastructure; tenant lease/capacity payments provide contractual economic support. Exact collateral/recourse, current subsidiary preferred ownership, property deed, utility terms and project cash-flow allocation remain incomplete. Company segment revenues are not automatically PF1-only AI revenue. The original approximately15-year lease terms are not interpolated into quarterly revenue. Building labels differ across disclosures, so native labels and source periods are retained.

**DO NOT ADD:** $2.35bn notes + refinanced SMBC debt; $1.59bn notes + repaid $300m bridge; platform preferred ceiling + campus commitment; lease contract value + debt + construction estimate. PF2/Delta Forge financing or utility contracts are not PF1 evidence.

## Comparative accounting limits

A consolidated developer, an unconsolidated hyperscaler JV and a corporate-developed campus can all bear contractual exposure through different entities. Nonconsolidation does not prove nonrecourse; a completion guarantee is not maximum expected loss. Security principal, equity contribution, carrying value, construction estimate and rent commitment describe different concepts. All three cases have unresolved project cash-flow/capital attribution. There is no AI ROI, adjusted leverage, synthetic investment total or systemic-credit conclusion.

The [October2 official PF1 release](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/161/applied-digital-brings-an-additional-75-mw-of-ai) reports250MW operational critical IT load. Its web text was reviewed after the June30 milestone; publisher bytes could not be archived following the host-access block. Both dated claims remain separate, with the newer one `PRIMARY_PARTIAL` and an explicit raw-source gap. Neither changes the400MW contract scope.
