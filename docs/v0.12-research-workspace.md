# V0.12 — Research workspace and data provenance

The release connects existing evidence into two usable Research tools. It adds no runtime APIs, credentials, forecasts or synthetic observations.

## Dataset scope

The workspace reads the original validated snapshots: headline CPI (1), drivers (7), dollar/fiscal monitor (10), AI/productivity (16), bank deposits (1), GPR (3), GSCPI (1), FAO (6) and SIPRI (4): **49 series**. Each retains its original publisher, ID, units, frequency, adjustment, observation dates, release/retrieval dates, source URLs, licensing, proxy flags and source flags when supplied. FRED is the distribution layer where specified; GPR is the authors' research index, not a government measurement.

Country panels remain in Map/Compare. CBO's historical and conditional projection paths remain in Fiscal. Digital-money publication estimates remain in their dedicated page; they are not converted into comparable time series. The derived GDP-per-worker calculation remains in AI & Productivity.

## Features and methodology

- `#/research/data`: bilingual search, topic/frequency filters, local favorites, up to three selected indicators and three presets. Selected IDs and range persist locally; URL parameters take precedence. Malformed IDs are discarded, duplicates removed and selections capped at three. Empty selections remain empty on reload.
- Charts share calendar bounds with independent vertical scales. Original levels retain their source units. Eligible monthly/quarterly YoY compares exactly the same period one year earlier; missing or non-positive denominators produce missing output. Rate and GSCPI series are not reinterpreted as growth. Quarterly dates represent quarter-start months. Source fiscal/calendar definitions remain explicit in series definitions.
- Paths break on missing observations and missing monthly/quarterly/annual intervals. For daily/weekly data, recorded nulls break paths and absent spans over ten days also break paths; ordinary weekends are not interpolated observations. Panels are descriptive, not causal comparisons.
- Period inspection, paginated tables and per-panel CSV export use the same selected observations. CSV includes measure, original units, frequency, adjustment, status, proxy, source, source update, retrieval, license and source flag. Empty values remain empty and textual formula prefixes are escaped.
- `#/research/updates`: filter retained ledger entries by series and whether values changed; aggregate changes across those retained checks; inspect bounded before/after examples and export summaries. Repeated revisions count as separate recorded events, not distinct periods. Source samples do not reconstruct full historical vintages. Workflow links are supplied separately because a successful check can coexist with a failed deployment or email.
- BLS productivity, BEA GDP, Census construction and Fed H.4.1 pages connect to integrated series. EIA electricity-detail and Census BTOS links are explicitly candidates, not integrated observations. CPI, BEA and Fed release-calendar links point to publisher-maintained schedules. New destination pages were reviewed online for this release.

## Compatibility

Six primary navigation entries are preserved. Two secondary routes are added; no legacy route is removed or redirected. The homepage adds a compact research-shortcuts card below existing evidence. Source health remains available at `#/sources?focus=health`. All snapshots and fiscal calculation code are unchanged by this implementation. Scheduled daily checks, atomic validation, rollback and Pages deployment continue through the existing workflow.

Gmail authentication was restored after replacing the app password. The complete manual refresh run [#31](https://github.com/ccesm/world-inflation-lens/actions/runs/36217076753) passed build, deployment and daily-email jobs. This confirms workflow and SMTP success, not inbox placement. The journal is not an email status dashboard, and ordinary code pushes do not trigger mail tests.

## Validation

The required gates are `npm run build` and `npm run verify`. Verification includes all 21 routes in both languages, old routes, CBO boundaries, existing calculations and source checks, plus workspace persistence/share precedence, empty selections, exact-period YoY, non-positive denominators, path gaps, CSV provenance and journal filtering. Browser checks cover search, favorites across reloads, presets, measure changes, downloads, journal filtering and responsive light/dark presentation. Final execution results are recorded in the release handoff.

Next priority: split routes and heavy data for faster first load, then integrate one validated Census adoption dataset with definitions and a revision baseline. More source links are not a claim of more integrated observations.
